# what it almost said
Tap a piece in the answer and this box shows its **top five guesses** as bars — the one it chose and the four it passed over, with their odds.

Most of the time the runner-ups are boring: `.` versus `,`, `is` versus `was`. The interesting ones are where the *meaning* forks — `perceive` versus `create`, `yes` versus `no`, `Paris` versus `巴黎`. That last kind is real: the model holds the same idea in many languages at once and sometimes the other language nearly wins.

Nothing here is made up after the fact. These numbers come back from the model with the answer; the wall only draws them.

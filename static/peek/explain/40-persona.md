# who it speaks as
Before your question, the model is given one short paragraph of instructions — the **system prompt**. It is not a setting or a mode; it is just words placed at the top of the conversation, and every answer is predicted from those words plus everything said since.

Pick one on the right and the session restarts with that paragraph in front. Same model, same weights, different first paragraph. Here they are, word for word:

Try the same question under two of them. "Only if sure" will happily be sure and wrong; "Show your steps" often gets the same question right, because writing the steps down is the only scratch memory the model has.

The Backpack

What a chat assistant actually receives when you press send, and why long conversations get expensive and forgetful.

The assistant remembers nothing between messages. Every time you press send, the whole conversation goes with it. Watch the right-hand side: you type a short line, the model receives the entire backpack. This demo's backpack is deliberately small (8,192 tokens) so you can see it fill.

When the backpack is nearly full, the assistant compacts: it writes a summary of the conversation and throws the transcript away. The backpack shrinks. But a summary is a guess at what mattered. The later questions ask about details from the start; each answer gets a ✓ or ✗ for whether the detail survived. Open the compaction card to see why.
